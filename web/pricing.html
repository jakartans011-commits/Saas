Questions

Pricing FAQ

What happens if I go over my monthly event limit?

Ingestion returns a quota-exceeded response once you hit the limit for the month — events already logged stay intact and verifiable. Upgrade at any time and the new limit applies immediately, no need to wait for the next billing cycle.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrades and downgrades take effect on your subscription right away; your event quota and retention window update to match the new plan.

Can I add teammates on the Free plan?

No. Free is a single-user plan (Owner only). Upgrade to Pro to invite teammates with Admin or Member roles.

What happens to my events if I cancel?

Your tenant drops to the Free plan's quota and retention window — events beyond that retention window age out under the Free plan's schedule. Admin and Member accounts are suspended, not deleted, and become active again if you upgrade. On Free, only your oldest active API key keeps working; the others are suspended, not deleted, and work again if you upgrade.

Is retention the same as backup?

No. Retention controls how long events stay queryable through the API before they're purged. Backups are a separate, infrastructure-level safeguard against data loss.
